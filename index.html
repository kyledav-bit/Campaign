I

Reorganizing the Budget Where the money goes and why.

I want to bring more structure to how we organize the budget. A four-level framework that makes revenues, expenses, and trade-offs easy for anyone to follow.

Guiding Principles

Every line item has a clear purpose. If something isn't serving the team well, we reorganize around what matters most.

Revenue + Expenses

Meticulous bookkeeping. Every income source and every expenditure accounted for and easy to trace.

Activity-Based Items

Organized by what we do: tournaments, travel, social events, hotels, etc.

Priority Tiers

Every expense falls into one of three tiers, so we always know what to protect first and where there's flexibility.

Tier 1

Essential

Tournaments, seminars, food, travel

Tier 2

High Value

Tryout pubbing, photos, socials

Tier 3

Enhancement

Apparel, optional events

Top of the pyramid gets protected first

II

How the Board Should Work Clear roles, real coordination.

I want to build on what we have and add more coordination across the board. The co-captains and president handle team-wide competitive and logistical functions. The president makes sure the DTTM, BTP Director, and Social Chair all have what they need to do their jobs well.

President

Tryouts & Training

Rubrics, calendar, retention

Tournaments

Judges, venue, participants, trophies

Social

Events, partnerships, data

Weekly 1:1s

Fifteen minutes with each director every week. What's going well, what's stuck, what needs a decision. Problems get caught early.

Board Dashboard

A live Notion page that tracks all board activities, tasks, and budget in one place. Everyone can see where things stand.

Written SOPs

How to book travel, order demos, declare expenses: all of it written down so it's easy for anyone to pick up and run with.

President's Portfolio

Travel, scrimmages, and demonstrative orders all fall to the president. Captains should be focused on the team's competitive game, not logistics.

III

Rebranding from the Inside Out How people see us, and how we see ourselves.

Everyone who interacts with Mock Trial forms an impression of who we are. I want to make sure that impression is intentional.

Circle One

Internal Identity

Reworked brand design that feels more polished. More merchandise, and things like business cards and resume workshops that make membership feel valuable to prospective members.

Circle Two

The Website

A real website: team roster with bios, results and honors, alumni page, testimonials. Clean, modern, and something we're proud to share.

Circle Three

Campus Presence

Strategic mixers with pre-law organizations, debate, Model UN. Track what converts to tryout sign-ups for future administrations.
